## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
Diyandi Festival Event Organizers

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
Organizers will benefit from having tools that allow them to view venue-specific weather details and manage schedules during Diyandi. In Iligan, the weather tends to be quite unpredictable, and it is not unheard of for rain clouds to roll in during clear skies on a September afternoon, which coincides with Diyandi season.


---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
They are trying to manage event schedules, oversee multiple outdoor venue activities and performer line ups, and stage schedules.


---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
The weather during Diyandi season often poses a threat to event performance management. Rainfall can cause delays to performances, cancel events, and potentially inflict damage to high value equipment like speakers or stages.

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
A specialized mobile application for event management with an integrated live weather monitoring.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
It offers event managers the ability to track the weather and manage schedule changes in real time, giving them the possibility to move events or change venue setups accordingly before heavy weather causes delays or cancellations.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. Check the likelihood of rainfall in an area by viewing the localized weather forecast and radar.

2. Check the impact of rain on event performance by viewing event schedules in tandem with projected rainfall.

---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful.

### Quality 1: High Reliability and Real-Time Speed

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Rainfall often requires immediate action. Tools that offer speed and reliability are a necessity because they give organizers time to make critical decisions to ensure the safety of attendees and equipment while minimizing losses.

### Quality 2: Ease of Use (Clear Interface)

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Event organizers are people who work in dynamic conditions, and they need to be able to navigate through schedules and weather projections with ease to issue instructions and updates in a timely manner.

---

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

1. Collect user feedback and satisfaction metrics after the event, and analyze how well the system performed in terms of accuracy and ease of use.
2. Determine whether there were less event cancellations or delays, and if there was less damage to equipment and stages compared to previous years.
